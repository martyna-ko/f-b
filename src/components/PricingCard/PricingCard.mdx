# PricingCard

**Purpose:** shows one price option clearly — who it is for, how much, and what it covers (user flow step "Pricing").

<Canvas of={Stories.Highlighted} />
<Controls of={Stories.Kids} />

## Anatomy

Audience badge · optional highlight badge · option name · price (large, heading font) · what it covers.

## Variants

<Canvas of={Stories.Family} />
<Canvas of={Stories.Adults} />

## Usage

- ✅ Prices come only from `content/studio.json` — never type a price in a page.
- ✅ Highlight at most one option per audience.
- ✅ Always say what the price covers ("per month", "valid 1 month").
- ❌ No "Buy" button — payment happens in Fitssey. Put one booking link under the price list.

## Accessibility

`<article>` with an `<h3>`, so screen-reader users can move between options. The highlight is a text label, not colour only.
