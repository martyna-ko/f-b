# SiteFooter

**Purpose:** contact details, social links and the portfolio notice on every page (site map: "On every page").

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## Anatomy

White logo · address and email · social links · portfolio notice.

## Responsive

Stacked on mobile; three columns from 768px.

## Usage

- ✅ Address, email and links from `content/studio.json`; notice text from `content/*.json`.
- ✅ The portfolio notice must always be visible — it tells visitors this is a case study.

## Accessibility

- `<footer>` landmark; address in `<address>`.
- White text on black: 21:1. Links are underlined (not colour only); focus ring is white on black.
- Social links announce "(opens in a new tab)".
