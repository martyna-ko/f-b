# Accordion

**Purpose:** answers common questions without making the page long — used for the FAQ on the kids page.

<Canvas of={Stories.OneOpen} />
<Controls of={Stories.Collapsed} />

## Anatomy

Question row (text + plus/minus icon) · answer panel · divider.

## States

Collapsed · expanded (icon becomes minus) · keyboard focus (violet ring).

## Usage

- ✅ Questions users really ask (from user flows: age, cancellations, parking, height requirement, full classes).
- ✅ Short answers; link out for detail.
- ❌ Don't hide information needed to decide or book (prices, times, requirements) — show those on the page.

## Accessibility

Native `<details>`/`<summary>`: focusable, opens with Enter or Space, announced as expandable by screen readers, works without JavaScript. Touch target ≥ 44px.
